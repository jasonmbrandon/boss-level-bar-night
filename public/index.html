Boss Level

A monthly nerd night at an LA bar or restaurant: tournaments with posted prizes, cosplay karaoke and retro games. Los Angeles unlocks at 100 players.

Hour by hour

Boss Level takes over a bar or restaurant on its slowest weeknight. Doors at 7, finals by 11, drink specials all night.

7:00 pm

Free play

CRTs, couches and the classics. Local game shops bring the systems and sell their stock at a table by the door.

Bracket

Double elimination on the big screen. Entry is posted with each event, and the prize is fixed before signups open.

Cosplay karaoke

Mic up in character. Theme songs, anime openings, boss battle anthems. Best costume picks the next song.

Grand finals

Finals on the main screen, prizes handed over on the spot, and next month's date announced before last call.

Questions, answered

Is it 21 and over?

It depends on the venue. Some nights are at bars and are 21+; others are at restaurants with a bar and are open to everyone, with ID checked for drinks. The age rule is posted with each event.

What does it cost to get in?

Walking in is free. Tournaments carry a small entry posted with each event; trivia, karaoke and free play cost nothing.

Where do the prizes come from?

Prizes are fixed and announced before signups open. Boss Level and its sponsors pay them, not the entry fees, and there's no betting on matches.

Do I have to cosplay?

No. Come as you are. A costume just gets you the karaoke mic first.

When does LA get a date?

When the Los Angeles count reaches 100. Then we book a venue, set the date and email this list before anyone else hears.

Can it come to my city?

Yes. Type your city when you sign up. Every city keeps its own count, and the next one to 100 is next on the map.

What's the Discord for?

Finding teammates, practicing before brackets, voting on games and hearing about new cities first.

I run a venue or a game shop.

Choose "I run a bar or restaurant" or "I run a game shop" when you sign up and we'll get in touch about hosting or sponsoring.
